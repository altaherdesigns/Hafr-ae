A pylon or monument sign stands on its own at the road or the gate. It names a building, a compound, a showroom or a station before anyone reaches the door. We fabricate them in our Fujairah workshop and install them on site.

## Pylon or monument

| Type | Shape | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pylon (totem) | Tall and narrow, often 3 to 8 m | Showrooms, petrol stations and malls seen from a fast road |
| Monument | Low and wide, about 1 to 2 m tall | Compound and building entrances, schools, villas |

A pylon is read from a moving car, so it needs height, large letters and usually light. A monument is read at walking or slow driving speed, at the gate, and can be more detailed.

## How they are built

A freestanding sign is a small structure. It has three parts:

1. **A foundation**, usually reinforced concrete, sized to the sign's height and the wind it must resist.
2. **A frame** of welded steel, galvanised or painted against rust.
3. **The cladding and lettering**: aluminium or stainless steel panels with cut or raised letters, lit or unlit.

This is fabrication work, the everyday work of our parent company, Al Taher Group, in its Fujairah workshops. Freestanding signs are always installed by our own team.

## Planning a freestanding sign

- **Check permission first.** Signs near a road usually need approval from the municipality and sometimes the roads authority. Your landlord or developer may have rules too.
- **Look for services.** Cables and pipes under the plot entrance must be found before the foundation is dug.
- **Plan the power.** A lit sign needs a cable run to its base before the concrete is poured.
- **Think about both directions.** A pylon beside a road is usually double-sided.

## Arabic and English

The building or company name usually appears in both languages. On a tall pylon, Arabic and English often sit one above the other; on a wide monument, side by side. A second Arabic reader checks every Arabic line before cutting.

## Frequently asked questions

**How tall should a pylon sign be?**
It depends on the road speed and what is around it. A sign on a fast road needs more height and larger letters than one at a compound gate. We suggest a size from your site photos.

Our own team, always. We agree the foundation and the cable run with you once we have seen the site.
