3D letters are cut one by one and fixed a little off the wall, so each letter has real depth and casts its own shadow. They are the most common choice for shopfronts, reception walls and building names, and they suit Arabic script especially well because every letter is cut to its true joined shape.

## Built letters and flat-cut letters

There are two ways to make a 3D letter.

- **Flat-cut letters** are cut from one thick sheet, 3 to 20 mm, and finished on the face and edges. They are crisp, strong and quick to make.
- **Built-up letters** have a face and a separate side wall, called a return, folded and joined around the letter's outline. They can be 30 to 100 mm deep, hollow inside, and can hold lights.

Flat-cut letters suit names at eye level and indoor walls. Built-up letters suit large shopfront and building names seen from the road, and any letter that needs to light up.

## How they are fixed

Each letter is held off the wall on threaded studs and spacers, usually 15 to 25 mm. The gap lets rain run off behind the letter, keeps the wall clean and creates the shadow line that makes the letters read as raised. We send a full-size paper template with every hole marked, so the letters go up in the right places, in the right order.

## Arabic letters need care

Arabic letters join and change shape with their position in the word. A logo redrawn badly, or a letter cut in its wrong form, is obvious to every Arabic reader. We cut from your typed text or your logo file, and a second Arabic reader checks the proof before anything reaches the machine. For styles of Arabic script, see our guide to [Arabic calligraphy styles](/blog/arabic-calligraphy-styles-family-name-sign/).

## Lit or unlit

Built-up letters can be lit from the front through an acrylic face, or from behind so light washes onto the wall around each letter. See [illuminated signs](/signage/illuminated-signs/) for the options.

## Frequently asked questions

**How thick should 3D letters be?**
Flat-cut letters are usually 3 to 20 mm thick. Built-up letters are usually 30 to 100 mm deep, depending on their height and whether they are lit.

**Can you cut our logo as well as the letters?**
Yes. Send the logo as a vector file (AI, EPS, SVG or PDF) for the cleanest cut. We can redraw a logo from a clear image, and you approve the redraw first.

**Which material is best outdoors?**
Stainless steel and powder-coated aluminium hold up best in Gulf sun and salt air. Acrylic works outdoors too, but some colours fade faster in direct sun.

**Do you install the letters?**
Yes, on request. Or we deliver them with a full-size template so your own team can fit them.
