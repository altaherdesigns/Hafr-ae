The reception wall is the first thing a visitor photographs in your office. We make logo walls, reception signs, door plates and directories in our Fujairah workshop, in Arabic and English, so every sign in the building matches.

## What we make for offices

- **Logo walls.** Your logo and name in raised letters behind the reception desk, lit or unlit.
- **Reception signs.** A panel with your name and logo, mounted on standoffs off the wall or glass.
- **Door and room plates.** Department names, meeting rooms, doctors' and managers' names.
- **Desk name plates.** Small engraved or cut plates for reception and desks.
- **Floor directories.** A board at the lift or entrance listing who is where. See [wayfinding signs](/signage/wayfinding-signs/).

## Materials

| Sign | Common choices | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Logo wall | Brushed or mirror stainless steel, brass, acrylic | Depth and a real shadow; can be halo-lit |
| Reception panel | Clear or frosted acrylic on standoffs, brushed stainless steel | A clean, light look on glass or plaster |
| Door plates | Brushed stainless steel, brass, engraved acrylic | Durable, easy to clean, easy to update |
| Directories | Aluminium or acrylic with changeable name strips | Tenants change; the board does not have to |

For the difference between raised letters and a single panel, see [stainless steel letters vs a plate](/blog/stainless-steel-letters-vs-plate/).

## One look across the building

The best office signage looks like one family: the same typeface, the same metal, the same mounting gap on every sign, from the logo wall to the meeting room door. We set the whole family on one proof, so you approve the system once instead of sign by sign.

## Arabic and English together

Government offices, clinics and many companies in the UAE show Arabic first. We set both languages together, size them to look equal, and have a second Arabic reader check every Arabic line before cutting. Our guide to [bilingual Arabic and English signs](/blog/bilingual-arabic-english-villa-sign/) covers the layout rules.

## How the job runs

1. **Send a photo** of the reception wall and your logo file, ideally a vector (AI, EPS, SVG or PDF).
2. **We send a proof** with sizes, materials and fixing positions marked.
3. **You approve it** in writing.
4. **We cut, finish and install**, usually outside your working hours if you ask.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can you make a logo wall from our logo file?**
Yes. A vector file gives the cleanest cut. If you only have an image, we redraw it and you approve the redraw first.

**Can the logo wall be lit?**
Yes. Built-up letters can be halo-lit, with a soft glow on the wall behind them. See [illuminated signs](/signage/illuminated-signs/).

**Can door plates be changed when staff change?**
Yes. We can make plates with a changeable insert, or cut a new plate to match the set.

**Do you make signs for clinics and pharmacies?**
Yes. Room names, doctor plates, directories and the shopfront sign, in Arabic and English.
