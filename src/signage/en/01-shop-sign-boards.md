A shop sign board is the one thing every customer sees before they see you. We make them in our workshop in Al Hail, Fujairah: raised letters on a fascia panel, lit or unlit, in Arabic and English, cut to the exact width of your shopfront.

## What a shop sign board is made of

Most shopfront signs combine three parts. You can choose each one separately.

1. **The fascia panel.** The flat board behind the letters. It covers the bulkhead above the shop window and gives the letters a clean background.
2. **The letters and logo.** Cut individually and raised off the panel, so they cast a shadow and read from across the street. See [3D letters](/signage/3d-letters/).
3. **The light.** None, light from the front, or light from behind the letters. See [illuminated signs](/signage/illuminated-signs/).

## Arabic and English on one board

Most UAE shop signs carry the business name in both languages. The two scripts need different heights to look equal: Arabic letters have tall rises and deep drops, so the Arabic line is usually set slightly larger than the English. We draw both lines together and check the balance on your proof.

Every Arabic line is read by a second Arabic reader against the text you typed before anything is cut. A wrong letter form on a shopfront is seen by every passer-by.

## How the job runs

1. **You send a photo** of the shopfront with the width of the fascia.
2. **We send a layout** with the letter heights and the panel size marked, set on your photo.
3. **You approve the proof** in writing.
4. **We cut, fabricate and finish** in the workshop.
5. **We install**, or deliver it ready to fit with a drilling template.

Many shop signs in the UAE need approval from the local municipality before they go up. The dimensioned proof we send is the drawing you will need, so check the requirements for your emirate before production starts.

## Sizing a shop sign

The sign should fill the fascia comfortably, with clear space at each end. As a starting point, keep the letters to about 60 to 70 per cent of the fascia's height, and leave at least one letter's height of space at each end. Our [house number size guide](/blog/house-number-size-visibility/) shows how letter height relates to reading distance; the same rule applies to a shopfront seen from the road.
