A lit sign keeps working after sunset, which in the UAE is when many shops do their best trade. There are three ways to light a sign, and each gives a different look. We build all three in our Fujairah workshop.

## Three ways to light a sign

| Type | How it works | Look | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Front-lit letters | LEDs inside a built-up letter shine through a translucent acrylic face | The whole letter glows | Shopfronts read from the road |
| Backlit (halo) letters | LEDs inside a letter with a solid metal face shine back onto the wall | A soft ring of light around each letter | Reception walls, restaurants, hotels, villas |
| Light box | A shallow box with LEDs inside and a printed or cut face | An evenly lit panel | Logos with fine detail, corner units, projecting signs |

Front-lit letters are the brightest and read from furthest away. Halo letters look calmer and more refined, but need a smooth, light-coloured wall behind them to show the glow. A light box is the simplest way to light a detailed logo. Our guide on [backlit vs non-backlit signs](/blog/backlit-vs-non-backlit-villa-sign/) shows the difference in photos.

## What a lit sign needs

- **Power within reach.** A cable to the sign position, ideally on a switch or a timer.
- **A driver that can be reached.** The driver turns mains power into the low voltage the LEDs use. It should sit where it can be serviced without taking the sign down.
- **Space behind halo letters.** Halo letters stand 30 to 50 mm off the wall so the light can spread.
- **A sealed build outdoors.** Rain, dust and heat are what make lit signs fail. Letters and boxes for outdoor use are sealed and drained.

Tell us on your first message whether there is power at the sign position. If there is not, an electrician will need to bring it there before we install.

## Colour of the light

Warm white suits wood, stone, sand-coloured walls and hospitality brands. Cool white looks sharper on modern glass and grey fronts. Coloured LEDs can match a brand colour, usually behind a coloured acrylic face. We note the light colour on your proof so there are no surprises.

## Arabic letters and light

Thin strokes and dots in Arabic script need care when lit. A front-lit letter must be wide enough to hold the LEDs, so very fine calligraphy may suit halo lighting better. We check this when drawing your proof and suggest a letter height that works.

## Frequently asked questions

**Which is better, front-lit or backlit letters?**
Front-lit letters are brighter and read from further away. Backlit halo letters look softer and suit reception and restaurant walls. Many shopfronts use front-lit letters for the name and a halo-lit logo.

**Can you light an existing sign?**
Sometimes. Built-up letters can often be fitted with LEDs; flat-cut letters cannot. Send us photos and we will tell you.

**Do lit signs need a permit?**
Often, yes, as with any shop sign. Check with your municipality or landlord.

**Can the sign switch on by itself at night?**
Yes. A timer or a light sensor on the circuit switches it on at dusk.
